**PEGG (Prime Editing Guide Generator)** designs prime editing guide RNAs (pegRNAs) at scale, including silent bystander mutations that improve editing efficiency and enable sequencing-based readout.

PEGG turns a list of desired edits into a ready-to-order oligo library, handling the design decisions — PBS and RT template lengths, nicking guides, sensor sequences — that otherwise have to be made by hand for every variant.

- Install: `pip install pegg`
- Code: [github.com/kexindon/PEGG3.0](https://github.com/kexindon/PEGG3.0)
