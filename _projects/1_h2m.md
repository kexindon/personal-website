**H2M (Human-to-Mouse)** is a Python package and database for the precision modeling of human genetic variants in mice, and vice versa.

Mouse models are the workhorse of cancer genetics, but translating a human mutation into the right mouse mutation is not straightforward: the genomes differ, the codons differ, and a naive coordinate lift-over often produces a variant with a different functional consequence. H2M solves this systematically.

### What it does

- **Ortholog querying** between the human and mouse genomes, including paralog-to-paralog mapping.
- **Variant translation** — generates murine equivalents of human variants that preserve sequence and functional consequences, and works in reverse.
- **Data ingestion** from common public mutation sources (ClinVar, cBioPortal, VCF).
- **A database** of over three million human–mouse mutation pairs, with base- and prime-editing libraries for thousands of variant pairs.
